## Stylus Nexus

We build and govern workflows for AI-assisted work. Control gates,
lifecycle loops, evaluation harnesses, and explicit decisions about where a
human stays in the loop.

**Start at [stylusnexus.github.io](https://stylusnexus.github.io)**: every
public project, one line each, grouped by what it's for.

If you only look at three:

- **[CritForge](https://www.getcritforge.com)**: tabletop RPG prep. 21
  generators, one shared memory, and contradiction detection across them.
  Commercial.
- **[Music Coach](https://stylusnexus.github.io/music-coach/)**: a free Mac
  app. Learn to make music and learn GarageBand, no instrument needed. MIT.
- **[Soundstellation](https://trees.soundstellation.com)**: a
  provenance-first graph of artist relationships. Every link sourced, limits
  shown. Free, in beta.
